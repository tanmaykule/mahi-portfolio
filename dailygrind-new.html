Daily Grind

SCOPE
Branding,
Social Media Design,
Visual Storytelling.
ABOUT
YEAR: 2024
Daily Grind is a vibrant and fun coffee brand offering four unique flavors that bring energy to your daily routine. Collaborating with this brand was an exciting and creative experience. For this project, I designed a three-panel promotional Instagram post showcasing the playful and colorful personality of the brand. The design format aligns with their ethos of making coffee an enjoyable and dynamic part of life.
Deliverables
Design Process
Why These Motifs?
The Vision Behind the Art
In creating these visuals, I focused on bringing out the fun and approachable character of Daily Grind. I used vibrant hues and quirky graphics to reflect the brand’s youthful energy. Every element, from the placement of the flavor icons to the text arrangement, was thoughtfully designed to engage their audience and leave a lasting impression.
When Form Meets Function
The illustration style is designed to be bubbly, with soft-edged motifs and a light color palette, reflecting the brand's core values of fun and vibrancy. The visuals showcase different moments in life, highlighting how coffee seamlessly fits into various lifestyles—whether for a college hustler, a corporate professional, a couple, or someone enjoying a quiet moment with their cat.
Why These Tones?
I used vibrant and bubbly hues to reflect the brand’s youthful energy. Each element has its own character and feel, making it relatable to the audience. Every outfit was thoughtfully curated to match the character's vibe, engaging the audience and leaving a lasting impression. This approach follows the principle of creating a sense of belonging and relatability through both colors and graphics.
