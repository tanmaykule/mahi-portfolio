YEAR: 2024
Radio Bar is a celebration of boldness, music, and opulence, a hotspot that thrives on its maximalist charm. For this project, I had the opportunity of crafting an entire series of stories for one of their vibrant musical nights. Each design was a symphony of vibrant colors, layered textures, and dramatic typography that perfectly captured the bar’s lively spirit.

The Vision Behind the Art
The stories and posts were designed to be as extravagant as the nights themselves—think bursts of boldness, intricate patterns, and playful, energetic elements that channel the brand’s unapologetically maximalist vibe. From bold graphics to intricate details, the visuals were crafted to immerse the audience in the high-energy atmosphere of the bar, ensuring every design was an experience in itself.
