Rococo Stamps

I created this illustration for Waliys, a Golti soda company, in 2025 as part of thier\ engagement marketing campaign for the overlapping festivals of Pongal and Makar Sankranti. The promotional post aimed to highlight how, regardless of the occasion, Waliys is always there to pair perfectly with any festive meal.

To bring this idea to life, I used the parallel illusion technique, creating a mirrored composition across both panels. While maintaining structural symmetry, I differentiated the essence of Pongal and Makar Sankranti through distinct clothing, accessories, supporting motifs, and color palettes. These details bring out the cultural identity of each festival while maintaining visual harmony.

The boy-girl duo subtly nods to the Kanu ritual, reinforcing familial bonds in the Pongal narrative, while the bhaylu-bhena (brother-sister) pairing reflects a similar sentiment seen in Gujarati traditions and films. Through this approach, the illustration not only celebrates regional flavors but also highlights the shared warmth of these festivals, making the connection between food, culture, and togetherness even stronger.
