ABOUT
YEAR: 2024
I created this illustration for Waliys, a Golti soda company, in 2025 as part of thier engagement marketing campaign for the overlapping festivals of Pongal and Makar Sankranti. The promotional post aimed to highlight how, regardless of the occasion, Waliys is always there to pair perfectly with any festive meal.

Design Process
The Defining Spark
Every design is the result of extensive research, decision-making, and careful consideration of its purpose and execution. However, there are moments when a design idea clicks effortlessly—like two perfectly fitting LEGO pieces. These "Eureka" moments can emerge unexpectedly, yet they have a lasting impact. No matter how many alternative concepts are explored, the initial idea remains the most compelling. This was precisely the case with the Walliyas design. The process began with numerous sketches and conceptual explorations, but one particular idea stood out. It resonated deeply with the team, and despite considering other directions, we continuously returned to this concept. Its clarity and strength made it the natural choice, and ultimately, we moved forward with it.
Why These Motifs?
The Vision Behind the Art
To bring this idea to life, I used the parallel illusion technique, creating a mirrored composition across both panels. While maintaining structural symmetry, I differentiated the essence of Pongal and Makar Sankranti through distinct clothing, accessories, supporting motifs, and color palettes. These details bring out the cultural identity of each festival while maintaining visual harmony.
Why These Tones?
The composition is anchored in a cold, dramatic color palette of midnight blues, shadowy greens, and smoky grays, accented with glowing crimson tones to evoke the dangerous allure of the Naagin.
