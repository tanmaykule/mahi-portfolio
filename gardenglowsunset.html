YEAR: 2024
Garden Glow Sunsets is a collection of promotional print and digital media posts and flyers designed for Hitansh Kulkarni, the founder and lead artist of his band.He curates events under the titles Garden Glow Sunsets and Electrosura.These deliverables were created as part of my freelance design collaboration with In designing the promotional materials for Garden Glow Sunsets.
Deliverables
Design Process
Why These Motifs?
The Vision Behind the Art
I focused on visually representing the fusion of surreal, electric, and classical music that defines the event. To achieve this, I incorporated elements like blooming flowers and tigers to symbolize natural beauty and strength, while swirling clouds and abstract hands evoke a surreal, dreamlike quality.
Why These Tones?
The vibrant color palette and bold typography were chosen to mirror the dynamic energy of electric music, while the organic shapes and classical-inspired motifs create a sense of balance and harmony. 
Bringing it all together
By blending these contrasting elements, I aimed to capture the essence of the music's fusion and translate it into a visual language that resonates with the audience, immersing them in the event's unique vibe even before they attend.
