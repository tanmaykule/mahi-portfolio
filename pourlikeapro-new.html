Pour Like a Pro

SCOPE
Publication design,
Print design,

ABOUT
YEAR: 2024
When we think of a cookbook, we picture vibrant food photos but, this one’s different. It’s all about alcoholic drinks! Designed for beginners, who are default to serving neat alcohol simply because they don’t know what else to make or how to pair drinks with the right occasion. So whether it’s a casual hangout, a big celebration, or a special toast, you’ll always have the perfect drink to impress your guests.

Design Process
Why These Tones?
The layout embraces a maximalist aesthetic with a pop-funky, clap-back energy—bold and unapologetic. The vivid color scheme of black, off-grey, yellow, and red enhances the vibrancy, resonating with the target audience. Initially, the plan was to use pink and black to strongly represent the punk vibe, drawing inspiration from the punk era. Howerver, while researching and considering drawing inspiration from alcoholic brands themselves, I came across the Budweiser 0.0 event.
Why These Motifs?
The eureka moment
As I analyzed the concept behind it, I had a eureka moment, one of its core initiatives was viewing smart drinking as an art form. The campaign promoted moderation, emphasizing hydration, food intake, and responsible drinking habits, ultimately aiming to shift perceptions around alcohol consumption. This perfectly aligned with my project, as I had already included a guidelines page for beginner drinkers and alcohol makers. I had also placed warning notes on each page, indicating how much one should drink and when to stop. It felt like the Budweiser campaign and my design vision were in sync—making it clear that this was the direction I wanted to take.
