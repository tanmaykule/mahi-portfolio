Ed Sheeran Concert

SCOPE
Visual Identity,
Promotional Design,
Music Event Branding
ABOUT
YEAR: 2024
A unique admit pass designed for the music festival, doubling as a collectible photo holder. Inspired by the Post-Impressionist technique of pointillism, the design pays homage to Ed Sheeran’s Photograph from the album Multiply, reinforcing the theme of preserving cherished memories.
Deliverables
Design Process
Frame the Moment
The Ed Sheeran 2024 India Tour concert tickets are more than just entry passes they’re keepsakes designed to preserve your memories. These innovative tickets come with a unique twist: a special photo slit where fans can insert a personalized Polaroid picture taken at the event’s exclusive photo booth. By paying a small fee at the booth, attendees can capture their experience, immortalizing the magic of the concert alongside their ticket.
