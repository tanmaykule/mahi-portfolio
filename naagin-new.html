ABOUT
YEAR: 2024
Naagin isn’t just a hot sauce brand—it’s a spellbinding experience, capturing the fiery essence of Indian mythology. I had to create a promotional illustration post inspired by the mythical Naagin—a serpent, a sorceress, a legend. This single post delves into the brand’s dark, gothic allure. It embodies the idea of how the serpent controls the snake’s brain in the same way the hot sauce has a captivating, long-lasting effect on our taste buds and our minds. Like being enchanted, even though it’s piping hot and spicy, one will still definitely want to keep coming back for more.

Design Process
Why These Motifs?
The Vision Behind the Art
The cobra, both deadly and commonly tamed by serpent charmers, represents a striking duality of power and control. Its presence is intensified by the third eye, a symbol of heightened perception and divine authority. This motif embodies strength and mysticism, creating a figure that is both commanding and enigmatic. Inspired by traditional aesthetics, the figure is dressed in a saree with gilded jewelry, blending cultural elegance with a bold presence. he composition explores the interplay between power and grace, control and chaos, danger and beauty—woven together into a singular, captivating vision.
Why These Tones?
The vibrant color palette and bold typography were chosen to mirror the dynamic energy of electric music, while the organic shapes and classical-inspired motifs create a sense of balance and harmony. 
Bringing it all together
By blending these contrasting elements, I aimed to capture the essence of the music's fusion and translate it into a visual language that resonates with the audience, immersing them in the event's unique vibe even before they attend.
