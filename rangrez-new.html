ABOUT
YEAR: 2024
Rangrez is a celebration of nature’s palette a playful, vibrant brand that creates organic pigments derived from real flowers. With each color, Rangrez invites you into a world where artistry and sustainability blend seamlessly. The visual narrative for Rangrez is centered around a chearfull explosion of color.
Deliverables
Why These Tones?
The design embraces a cheerful yet sophisticated palette, inspired by blooming marigolds, delicate hibiscus, and radiant bougainvillea. The playful, cheerful patterns evoke creativity and spontaneity.
A Wake Up Call
Power Of A Good Copy
The tagline, “make the right choice today,” that like a walking call for the parents. Rangrez isn’t just a pigment company; it’s a movement toward more sustainable and meaningful art-making. Whether it’s a canvas, a mural, or a simple craft project, Rangrez colors transform every surface into a celebration of life and nature.
Bringing It All Together
Allowing customers to create a custom packaging box for their children adds a delicate sense of personal connection. Instead of names, a simple handprint on the back becomes a quiet mark of identity, an organic, playful touch that feels both intimate and timeless. Thoughtfully designed with the child's age in mind, this detail transforms the packaging into more than just a box. This subtle detail ensures that every box feels uniquely theirs, a small but meaningful gesture that lingers beyond the product itself.
Key learnings
This project taught me not only the necessary skill set for building marketing and brand collaterals but also much more, like the core concepts of understanding stakeholders, creating value propositions, and developing hypothetical customer and consumer profiles. I also learned important concepts, such as understanding target audience and the fact that sometimes the customer and the consumer belong to different markets and need to be targeted and handled separately.
